# RUNBEN Children's Water-based Peel-Off Nail Polish Information

This is a product information and safety guide for a series of water-based peel-off nail polishes intended for children, essential for safe usage and proper product maintenance.

## Product Specifications

Overview of the available product variants and their respective regulatory filings.
- The line includes five variations (W01 through W05) with minor differences in trace ingredients like colorants.
  > Ingredients lists and trace ingredient listings for W01-W05
- All products are water-based peel-off formulas intended for children aged 3 and above.
  > Product title and Caution #3
- The product batch KG358005 has an expiration date of December 14, 2028.
  > Batch No.: KG358005, Exp.: 2028/12/14

## Usage Instructions

Procedures for the correct application and maintenance of the nail polish.
- Apply to clean, dry nail surfaces, applying in thin layers with drying time between coats.
  > How to Use #1, #2, #3
- Avoid water exposure, including bathing or swimming, for 4 hours after application.
  > Caution #1
- If the formula separates due to long storage, shake the bottle before use.
  > Caution #7
- If the product freezes below 3°C, thaw it in a room-temperature area or warm water; do not force open while frozen.
  > Caution #8

## Safety and Storage

Important safety guidelines to prevent accidents and maintain product integrity.
- Use only under adult supervision and ensure it is kept out of the reach of infants.
  > Caution #4, #5
- For external use only; do not ingest.
  > Caution #3
- Store in a cool, dry place and tighten the cap promptly after use.
  > Caution #6, Storage Conditions
